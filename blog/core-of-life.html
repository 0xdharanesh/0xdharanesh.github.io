The Core Pillars of Life are: 1. Health (Physical + Mental) 🏋️‍♂️🧠 The foundation—without it, nothing else matters. Physical: Fitness, nutrition, rest. Mental: Peace, emotional well-being, stress management. 2. Wealth (Financial + Intellectual + Purpose) 💰📚🎯 Financial: Stability and resources to live comfortably. Intellectual: Knowledge, skills, innovation. Purpose: A meaningful pursuit that keeps you growing. 3. Relationships (Personal + Social) ❤️🤝 Personal: Love, family, deep friendships. Social: Community, networking, teamwork. Life feels empty without meaningful connections. This forms the core of life—everything else builds on top of it. Freedom, experiences, and choices are rights, but these three pillars sustain and fulfill life itself.
